# THRIVE student support

THRIVE supports university students through check-ins, public guidance and consent-based referral. The Test experience uses clearly labeled synthetic examples.

## Language

**Student — الطالب**:
A person using their university's support experience. A student's check-ins, choices and referral requests belong to that student within their university.

**University service — الخدمة الجامعية**:
A university-scoped source of counseling or specialist support with its own availability and access information.
_Avoid_: Appointment, clinician account

**Referral — الإحالة**:
A consent-based request to a support service, with a tracked handling status. A request is not a confirmed appointment.
_Avoid_: Booking confirmation, completed treatment

**Service-use consent — موافقة استخدام الخدمة**:
The student's agreement to use the service under a particular policy. It is separate from research participation.

**Research consent — موافقة المشاركة في البحث**:
The student's optional agreement to research participation. Declining it does not prevent service use.

**Referral-sharing consent — موافقة مشاركة الإحالة**:
The student's agreement to the minimum sharing needed for a specific referral request.

**Check-in session — جولة المتابعة**:
A student's resumable set of responses to a versioned instrument, ending in a recorded result when completed.
_Avoid_: Sign-in session, diagnosis

**Demonstration — الجولة التجريبية**:
A synthetic product-usability check-in used to test the experience. Its technical total has no psychological or diagnostic meaning.
_Avoid_: Clinical assessment, risk category

**Guide resource — مورد إرشادي**:
A versioned piece of public guidance with a publication state and source. Downloading public guidance does not mean storing student responses.

**Daily feeling — الشعور اليومي**:
An optional self-reported feeling for a particular day. It is not a clinical instrument or an automatic risk signal.

**Privacy suppression — الحجب لحماية الخصوصية**:
Withholding a sensitive aggregate when its group is below the approved minimum. A withheld count does not mean zero.
_Avoid_: Missing score, zero participants
